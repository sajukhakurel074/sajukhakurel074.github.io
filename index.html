EMBEDDED SYSTEMS · TINYML · IOT

Embedded intelligence for small sensing devices.

I'm Saju Khakurel, an embedded systems engineer with three years of firmware experience on electric vehicles and charging stations. I hold an Erasmus Mundus master's degree (EMINENT, grade 1.6; thesis 1.0) and build machine learning and firmware pipelines for resource-constrained devices.

Research interests: TinyML toolchains, wearable sensing and IoT data pipelines. I am looking for a PhD position in embedded and edge AI.
